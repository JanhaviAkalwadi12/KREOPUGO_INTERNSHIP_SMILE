Why SMILE Program?

Building confident, responsible and emotionally strong students.

Why This Program?

This program aims at addressing the challenges faced by today's adolescents.

Low Attention Span
Emotional Confusion
Screen Addiction
Exam Pressure
Poor Communication

Why Life Skills Are Important

70%

Around 70% of learning difficulties in adolescents are linked to emotional and behavioral challenges rather than intellectual ability.

50%+

Over 50% of adult behavioral patterns are formed during early adolescence (ages 11–14).

CBSE has mandated skill education for Classes 6–8 with 110 hours each year, emphasizing real-life competencies.

Mode of Training

Online + Offline

We have a blend of online and offline mode of imparting training.

SMILE Curriculum

The SMILE curriculum is designed in alignment with CBSE’s focus on Life Skills, Values Education and NEP 2020’s emphasis on holistic learner development.


CBSE has made it compulsory now to give the life skills sessions to the students. Approximately 1700 CBSE schools are there in Karnataka. It’s need of the hour to provide them with qualified trainers to give the life skills training.

By the End of the Academic Year

Students will be able to:

Understand and manage emotions
Communicate confidently
Develop discipline and responsibility
Improve focus and study habits
Build self-esteem and resilience
Make better choices in academics and life
