About Us

Program Vision and Leadership

The SMILE - Life Skills Program is conceptualized and guided by Mr. Mahesh Masal, a committed educationist and youth development professional with a strong vision of nurturing emotionally strong, confident, and responsible students.

Academic & Training Leadership

The program is led and coordinated by Ms. Nanda Hampiholi, Lead Trainer and Program Head, who brings extensive experience in life skills training, communication, confidence building, and NLP-based learning methodologies.

Training Team

The sessions are delivered by a team of trained and certified facilitators. Each trainer follows a structured curriculum, activity-based methodology, and student-centric approach to maintain uniformity and effectiveness.

Our Commitment

We are committed to helping students SMILE with confidence: by building Self-awareness, Motivation, Inner strength, Life skills, and Emotional balance.
